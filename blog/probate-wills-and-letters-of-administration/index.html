Succession

Probate,wills,andlettersofadministration

Taking out letters of administration, preparing testamentary dispositions, and the drafting and custody of wills — succession as it is actually practiced.

Probate, as practised in these chambers, includes taking out letters of administration, the preparation of testamentary dispositions, and the drafting and custody of wills. It is family work, and it is land work, and it is almost never only one of those. A grant that is not confirmed cannot distribute. A will that is not attested cannot be proved. A dependant who is omitted does not disappear. The Law of Succession Act is particular, and the probate registry is more particular still. We advise on the making of a will while the testator can still speak, and we petition for grant when they cannot. Custody of the will is held in confidence at chambers.
