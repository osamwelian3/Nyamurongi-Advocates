The Firm

Achambersforthelongmatter.

Advocates of the High Court of Kenya. A Kisii chambers practising litigation, probate, conveyancing, and commercial counsel — with a standing record before the courts of Nyanza and beyond.

Nyamurongi & Company Advocates is a Kisii chambers of advocates of the High Court of Kenya. The practice is organised into four working divisions — litigation, probate, conveyancing, and commercial & corporate — with a substantial additional caseload in employment and land.

From Lengetia Place on Moi Highway, counsel instruct and appear across Nyanza and the wider Rift: Kisii, Kilgoris, Kericho, Kisumu, Nakuru and Nairobi. The chambers have been on the public record for institutional clients including Kisii University, Kisii Bottlers, Family Bank, and construction and trading houses, as well as private persons in succession and property.

The firm's public word is plain. Law, as we have put it, sets the standard for acceptable conduct and supplies the framework for resolving dispute. Without it, people are left to their own devices. Our work is to keep that framework honest for the client who instructs us.
